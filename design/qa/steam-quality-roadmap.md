## P1 — next gameplay depth

- **First employee slot — complete.** Three hinted candidates now expose different risk/reward profiles, reveal their traits through live events, appear as distinct Blender-authored models tied to their behavior, charge visible wages, and persist through resume/campaign statistics.
- **Endless contract loop — complete.** Day 15+ nights offer three paid, previewable mastery contracts with live HUD progress, explicit success/failure settlement, bounded difficulty, partial refunds and persistent shop-sign stamps/ranks. Success remains a 78–122 coin sink instead of another income multiplier; the ordinary star goal caps at 380 so endless mode cannot become mathematically impossible.
- **Recipe collection and directed research — complete.** The former count-only night collection is now a 10+3 entry journal. Discovered entries expose exact bilingual formulas; unknown entries expose unlock-aware authored clues and can become a persistent research target that completes through the real trial loop.
- **Research-to-operation menu loop — complete.** Legendary and graded custom recipes retain formulas, price, tier, heat and sales. Players choose three next-day features; real customers order them at menu price, fresh launches increase traffic and heat decays into a slower evergreen cadence. Tomorrow's event now exposes recipe-fit bonuses and matching drinks receive double demand weight plus a visible per-sale reward, preventing the former permanent highest-price trio from dominating every event. Invisible passive income remains removed.
- **Dark-menu operation loop — complete.** Dark legends and failed experiments retain orderable formulas in three separate novelty slots. The night board previews price/formula/real sales, supports deliberate unlisting, and turns a full discovery into an explicit candidate-versus-slot replacement decision. One real dark order rotates every five ordinary arrivals; completed cups award +2 buzz when clean and not overcooked or +1 otherwise. Occupying slots grants nothing at day end. Settled buzz is snapshotted into capped next-day traffic and persistent inspiration.
- **Parallel tea/pearl preparation slice — complete.** Tea stock is tracked per base and pearls use a separate portion queue. Their 8s/10s clocks run concurrently through golden, late and overcooked windows while normal cup input remains active. Golden portions add +2 coins and +1 inspiration; overcooked portions lose ¥2 and cannot count as Perfect. The full flow is touchable and has Q/E plus Xbox LB/RB, PlayStation L1/R1 and Nintendo L/R shortcuts, a dedicated first-day tutorial step, fixed-clock campaign-bot participation and independent model/scene regression gates.
- **Repeat-trial growth loop — complete.** Every valid night trial reveals one tea/milk cell exactly once, while every used ingredient advances a visible five-level mastery card. Lv3+ strengthens that ingredient's main flavor; completing all 12 pairings permanently adds two nightly inspiration, with an immediate two-point refund when the final cell is revealed.
- **Short-term lab briefs — complete.** One visible brief converts the long affinity/mastery horizon into an executable current step: reveal an available pair, practice an under-Lv3 ingredient twice, or make a 75+ quality cup after the collections mature. Incomplete work persists across nights; completion refunds one trial cost and cannot issue or pay a second brief on the same day.
- **Market-test payoff and integrity — complete.** The former long-text sleep popup is now a two-column staged reveal with score, 1/3–3/3 progress, average, trend, tier, menu price and failure refund. The ±8 variance is deterministic for a drink/day/stage, settlement is saved exactly once, and reopening the night restores the locked result instead of rerolling or returning to the lab.
- **Operating-cost decision loop — complete.** Every sold cup carries a 50%-wholesale stock cost, while wrong cups and player-initiated discards expose waste in the same ledger. The reset control previews its exact current loss; system resets stay free. Wages, stock and weekly rent resolve into a five-column net ledger while stars remain tied to gross shift performance. Rent is previewed during play, rises from 40 on Day 7 to a 120 cap, and can consume at most 25% of post-stock cash; explicit startup relief prevents debt and a novice death spiral.
- **Staff-wage integrity and recovery — complete.** A staff member no longer keeps every hook after receiving a partial wage. The till pays what it has, the shortfall schedules one clearly messaged rest day with no wage or debt, all positive and negative trait hooks pause together, and the employee automatically returns the following day. Staff save data is rebuilt from authored candidates so a corrupt save cannot forge wage, stats or trait identity.
- **Fourteen-day capital strategy — complete.** A persistent strategy harness now drives the real day scene, cup workflow, operating ledger, upgrades, staff, menu research, weekly rent and campaign report across all fourteen days. It exposed the old 1,740-coin tree as a single-day-balanced price sheet that every route completed by Day 10. Entry prices remain 60/70/80, while compounding mid/late tiers bring the tree to 2,930; the night panel previews tomorrow's wage, rent and safely investable balance. Conservative / balanced / growth routes now resolve to 9/10 + A, 10/10 + S and 10/10 + S with materially different cash buffers.
- **New Shop+ strategy separation — complete.** The three sign routes share inherited progression and an 8% goal increase but expose different execution tests: 12% faster arrivals with an every-third-cup payout, 12% lower patience with fast-service payout, or 6% traffic per hot feature with trend-match payout. A rejected first pass let Turnover generate 327 bonus coins. Route-specific second-week calendars now drive 10 / 10 / 11 real menu rotations; final cash remains within 5.9%, and all routes still reach 10/10 upgrades and S.

Unlock requires a Godot template with a true arm64 simulator slice (or building the template from source); a device install with the existing development signature remains the alternate path.

## Exit gate

The project reaches the intended quality bar when a fresh player can complete the full 14-day campaign, encounter enough variation to make repeated days meaningfully different, find the verified audio mix comfortable over a full run, resume safely from every saved phase, and pass keyboard/gamepad/touch plus target-platform performance checks.

## Balance watch

Reworked-but-correct cups retain their full menu price, service tip, event reward and staff reward; independently qualified Precision/Trend route payouts remain, while Turnover's explicitly combo-based third-cup payout resets with the combo. Only the one-point clean-build inspiration and Perfect count are otherwise withheld. A novice can therefore correct a recipe without losing operating revenue, while mastery finally produces a distinct reward signal. Manual discard adds only the already-authored 50%-wholesale basket cost and remains covered by the same no-debt settlement relief. Human sessions should verify whether the first-day tutorial makes this distinction clear without making cautious players afraid to correct mistakes.

The error-aware autoplay now separates order RNG from a reproducible `BOT_SAMPLE` craft RNG. Across five samples each for Day 1/5/9/12 and newbie/regular/skilled profiles (60 real 120-second shop scenes), every run kept 100% service and positive operating net. Newbie averages were 2.0/2.2/1.4/2.0 stars with 73%/71%/80%/72% clean rates; Day 9's worst point still produced 1 star, 224 gross and 156 operating net. Regular averaged 83–89% clean and reached 3 stars from Day 5 onward; skilled averaged 98–100% and held 3 stars. This proves the clean-craft reward creates separation without a zero-star or cash death spiral in the sampled upgrade path; human testing remains necessary for perceived fairness.

Skilled players can exceed 600 coins on group and contract days because each ticket creates an extra paid cup. The campaign report rewards balances up to 900 coins and New Shop+ resets the operating economy. Endless contracts now remove 78–122 coins even on success and 120–190 on failure, but human sessions must still judge whether those losses feel aspirational rather than punitive before introducing more income multipliers.

Three fresh featured recipes add 15–38 coins in unupgraded Day 9/12 deterministic runs and up to 12% arrival pressure. They do not rescue the Day 12 newbie from 0★ without growth and do not grant the Day 12 regular a free third star. Human sessions should verify that the traffic pressure is legible and that players rotate recipes instead of treating the first three slots as permanent.

The completed affinity notebook adds only two nightly inspiration: one additional trial after twelve distinct base/milk experiments. It does not modify daytime income or traffic. Human sessions should verify that discovery cadence feels like learning rather than compulsory grid completion and that Lv3 flavor changes remain understandable when a familiar recipe crosses the threshold.

A lab brief can return exactly two inspiration once per operating day, and only after spending a real trial toward its objective. It therefore changes the opportunity cost of one directed experiment rather than creating an unlimited resource source. Incomplete briefs carry forward instead of being discarded, preventing daily rerolls or deliberate failure from becoming optimal.

Market-test variance remains the authored ±8 range and a 300-sample regression reaches both endpoints with a centered mean. Deterministic locking removes save-scumming without changing the three-night thresholds (88 hit, 78 signature, 62 regular) or the three-inspiration failure refund.

The independent operating-ledger replay still establishes the 69.7% / 71.6% / 74.4% margin floor. The integrated fourteen-day strategy replay now also rotates featured recipes against tomorrow's event: conservative rotates 8 times and ends at 9/10 upgrades, 174 coins and A; balanced rotates 12 times, reaches 10/10 on Day 11, retains 1,516 and earns S; growth rotates 8 times, reaches 10/10 on Day 10, retains 3,237 and earns S. This is deliberately a mastery spread, not forced poverty. Five human campaigns should still test whether menu forecasting is understood without becoming nightly busywork and whether a 520–600-coin final upgrade feels aspirational.

The same balanced fourteen-day policy started New Shop+ with the S-grade 80 coins / 3 inspiration inheritance and used each route's Day 9+ calendar. Turnover, Reputation and Trend finish with 6,033 / 5,962 / 5,903 gross, 1,753 / 1,732 / 1,656 cash, and 105 / 159 / 78 attributable route bonus. All finish the upgrade tree on Day 10 and score S; Reputation and Trend land at 41/42 stars because their reordered late events expose different target pressure. The maximum cash spread is 97 coins, or 5.9%. On deterministic newbie Day 12 before the calendar change, the routes remain at 1 star with 15–16 served cups; a future human replay should focus on whether the new sequence feels like a district identity rather than an arbitrary difficulty shuffle.

On deterministic regular Day 10, Acheng on duty produces 322 gross / 264 operating net after the 14-coin wage; the forced rest state produces 305 / 250 and matches the solo 305 / 249 within one randomized stock coin. Rest therefore removes both the +2 quality check and the extra-shake cost instead of leaking a hidden advantage. Shortfall remains a catastrophic-shift recovery state rather than a routine optimization because wages settle before stock and rent.
